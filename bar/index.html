Event Service Pricing

Bartender Service Pricing

Based on Number of Guests

  • Total Hours of Event Time: 4 (minimum)

    The "Total Hours of Event Time" is how long the bartender will be at the location. This includes 1.5 hours of set-up time plus the time the bartender will serve drinks and the time it will take him/her to breakdown and clean for departure. For instance: If your event starts at 6:00pm and ends at 8:30pm, we will arrive at 4:30pm to set up, start service at 6:00pm, slowly start closing at 8:15pm and finally stop pouring drinks at 8:30pm and depart.

  • Number of Bartenders: 1 bartender for every 75 guests for the best service.

  • Travel Fee (may apply based on distance) — No travel fee for events within a 20-mile radius of bartender location. A one-way travel fee of $1.00 per mile applies outside the 20 miles radius, up to 30 miles. $1.50 per mile after 30 miles.

  • Tip Jar: If NO tip jar is provided, a $100 gratuity per bartender applies. Not included in the price quote and payable to each bartender at the end of service.

  • Last-Minute Booking: A $100 fee may apply if you book within 7 days prior to your event, regardless of when you receive a quote.

Cancellation & Deposit Policy

To reserve your event date, we require a 30% deposit. If you need to cancel for any reason, 15 days prior to your event, we will refund your deposit minus a 10% "SAVE MY DATE" fee. If you were to cancel within 10 days of your event, your initial deposit is non-refundable. The final 70% payment is due two days prior to your event.

Some events may require more set-up and breakdown time. We quote for 1.5 hours of set-up before the event and .5 hours breakdown time after the event. The amount of set-up time may fluctuate for your event, and we will address this as your date approaches.

PRICING GUARANTEE: The pricing in this proposal is good for 30 days after it is received. Once a deposit is received, pricing is secured. After 30 days we will send you a new proposal if needed.

CONTRACTED TIMES: The "set-up" time (1.5 hours typically) on the contract is when your bartender(s) will arrive to set up for the event, the "start" time is when we will be prepared to start pouring drinks and the "end" time is when we stop pouring drinks. We also bill for .5 hours of clean-up time. You can request your bartender(s) to stay longer during the actual event, and we will add their hourly charge which will be paid immediately upon the event.
